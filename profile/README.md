## Hi there 👋

We are **United Campus Workers Colorado**, CWA Local 7799 — a wall-to-wall union representing workers across Colorado's public higher-education system. Our members include part-time and full-time staff, faculty, and graduate and undergraduate workers at the University of Colorado and Colorado State University. We organize together because the conditions we share at work are bigger than any single job title, classification, or campus.

Our mission is to champion and defend the interests and well-being of all University labor, and to build and sustain social and economic justice in our workplaces and our communities.

### What lives in this organization

This GitHub organization is the public home for the technical and collaborative work that supports our union — public documents, data and research projects, member-facing tools, and templates that organizers and chapter committees can reuse. Repositories here are public unless they contain member data. If you maintain or contribute to one of our projects and have a question, open an issue on the relevant repository or reach the elected leadership through the [contact page](https://www.ucwcolorado.org/contactus).

### A few of our wins

Since forming in Spring 2020, our members have organized to win yearly raises for graduate workers, dental coverage at CU Boulder, the CU Boulder graduate fee waiver (worth $1,000–$2,000 a year), 8% raises for student workers at UCCS, 3% raises for faculty and staff, hazard pay for CU Boulder Resident Advisors, and a delay in budget cuts at CU Denver. At the state level, we helped pass **SB23-111, the Public Workplace Protection Act**, which secured the right of Colorado public employees to organize, to engage in concerted activity, and to participate in political life off the clock without retaliation. We have also won seats on the Boulder and Denver city councils. A fuller account is on the [Who We Are](https://www.ucwcolorado.org/whoweare) page.
